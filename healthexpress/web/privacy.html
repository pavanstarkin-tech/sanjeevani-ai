Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

HealthExpress AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy and health data. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection when you use our telehealth consultations, emergency pharmacy delivery, and ABDM-integrated healthcare platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or sign in via Google OAuth or Firebase Authentication, we collect the following:

  • Identity Data: Full Name, Email Address, and Google Profile Picture.
  • Contact Details: Phone Number, Delivery Address, and Emergency Contact details.
  • Clinical & Health Records: Aarogyasri Health Pass ID, prescription records, consultation history, and vitals snapshots provided voluntarily for clinical assessments.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure Firebase OAuth credentials to maintain encrypted session states.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and securely verify your identity for telemedicine sessions and clinical records.
  • To route prescription orders and medical supplies from authorized partner pharmacies in your locality.
  • To provide AI-assisted health triage and symptom assessments powered by medical models.
  • To generate official digital clinical consultation records and secure prescriptions.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal health data to third-party advertisers.
  • Your data is only shared with licensed doctors and verified pharmacy partners strictly for fulfilling your requested healthcare services.
  • All network communication is encrypted end-to-end via TLS/SSL, adhering to HIPAA and ABDM security guidelines.

4. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

HealthExpress AI complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We strictly use your Google profile (name, email, photo) solely for authenticating your user account in our application.
